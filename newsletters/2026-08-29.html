Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms your portfolio managers and analysts use daily — built around the themes in today's edition: autonomous AI agents, overnight screening, and measuring efficiency in earnings.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares and selling them, expecting to buy them back cheaper. Profits when a stock falls. AI overnight screening tools now flag short-squeeze risk before markets open.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value. A key downside risk metric. AI portfolio tools monitor drawdown in real time, alerting PMs before a threshold is breached.
Liquidity
How quickly an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price. Low liquidity means large trades shift the market against you — a critical constraint AI execution tools must respect.
Bid-Ask Spread
The gap between the price a buyer will pay and the price a seller will accept. Wider spreads mean higher transaction costs. AI market-making tools tighten spreads on liquid assets.
Active vs. Passive
Active managers try to beat a benchmark through stock selection; passive funds track an index. AI is blurring the line — quantitative active strategies now scale like passive, at lower cost.
Tracking Error
How closely a portfolio follows its benchmark — measured as standard deviation of excess returns. Lower tracking error = more index-like. A key metric when evaluating AI-driven systematic strategies.

EU AI Act · August Deadline

EU AI Act High-Risk Deadline Has Landed — Banks Without Conformity Assessments Are Exposed

August 2, 2026 was the binding compliance deadline for high-risk AI systems under the EU AI Act — and most AI used in banking qualifies. From this date, newly deployed high-risk systems require risk management systems, bias testing, technical documentation, human oversight mechanisms, and a conformity assessment before deployment. Fines reach €35M or 7% of global revenue.

Why It Matters

Any AI product you ship into a regulated EU context is now legally required to have documented risk management, human oversight, and bias testing baked in from day one — not retrofitted post-launch.
