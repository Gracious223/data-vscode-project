Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms you will hear from analysts and portfolio managers in the context of AI deployment — build this vocabulary and you will hold the room more confidently in product discovery conversations.

Short Selling
Borrowing and selling a security expecting its price to fall, then buying it back cheaper to pocket the difference. AI risk systems now flag unusual short interest spikes in real time.
Yield Curve
A graph of bond yields across maturities. An inverted curve (short rates above long) historically signals recession. AI nowcasting models parse yield curve signals as macro regime inputs.
Basis Points (bps)
One basis point = 0.01%. A fee of "25bps" means 0.25%. Essential for understanding fund costs, interest rate moves, and spread comparisons — used constantly by fixed income analysts.
Mark-to-Market (MTM)
Valuing a portfolio at today's market prices rather than purchase price, generating daily P&L. AI tools processing real-time data help PMs manage MTM exposure intraday.
Passive vs. Active
Passive funds track an index cheaply. Active managers try to beat it. AI is blurring the boundary — "smart beta" strategies use systematic, rules-based models rather than pure human discretion.
Liquidity Risk
The risk that an asset cannot be sold quickly at a fair price. Bonds and private assets carry higher liquidity risk than equities. AI models now simulate liquidity stress scenarios in seconds.

EU · AI Act

EU AI Act Enforcement Is Live — But the AI Omnibus Just Bought Finance an Extra Year

August 2 marked full enforcement of EU AI Act high-risk obligations. But the AI Omnibus — which entered into force July 27 — extended the Annex III deadline (covering credit scoring, fraud detection, and AML) to December 2, 2027. Firms with EU customers or regulated EU entities are still in scope for general obligations. Fines reach €15M or 3% of global revenue.

Why It Matters

The extension buys time, not permission. If your product touches credit, fraud, or access-to-services decisions for EU users, you need to know whether you are Annex III in-scope — and start documentation now, not in 2027.
