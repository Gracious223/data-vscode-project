Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your portfolio managers and analysts use these terms daily. Building fluency with them isn't just vocabulary — it's credibility that unlocks better product conversations. Six fresh terms today.

Beta
How sensitive a stock is to market moves. Beta of 1 = moves with the market. Beta of 1.5 = 50% more volatile than the index. AI tools that manage portfolio beta exposure are addressing one of the most fundamental risk levers.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A duration of 5 means the bond's price falls ~5% if rates rise 1%. Higher duration = more rate risk. AI tools that monitor portfolio duration in real time are high-value in rising-rate environments.
Credit Spread
The yield difference between a corporate bond and a government bond of the same maturity. Wider spreads signal higher perceived risk. AI models that monitor spread movements across thousands of issuers simultaneously give analysts a significant edge.
EBITDA
Earnings Before Interest, Taxes, Depreciation and Amortisation. A proxy for operating cash flow used to compare companies across capital structures. Analysts spend significant time modelling EBITDA trajectories — a core candidate for AI-assisted financial modelling.
Net Asset Value (NAV)
The per-share value of a fund's assets minus its liabilities. The benchmark price at which fund shares are bought and sold. For AI products in asset management, NAV accuracy and speed of calculation is a core infrastructure dependency.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Net income divided by shares outstanding. One of the most-watched metrics in equity analysis — beats or misses vs. analyst consensus move stock prices significantly. AI tools that track EPS revision trends are widely used in systematic strategies.
