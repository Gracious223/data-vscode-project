Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's six terms cover the vocabulary underpinning how your analysts think about returns, risk, and market structure — each one surfaces in the context of AI-powered research and portfolio tools.

Sharpe Ratio
A measure of risk-adjusted return: how much excess return (above the risk-free rate) a portfolio earns per unit of risk taken. AI portfolio tools optimise for Sharpe, not raw returns alone.
Bid-Ask Spread
The gap between what a buyer will pay and what a seller will accept. A narrow spread signals a liquid, efficient market; a wide spread means higher transaction costs. AI market-making compresses spreads.
Market Capitalisation
Total market value of all outstanding shares (price × shares issued). Large-cap, mid-cap, and small-cap are defined by this figure — and shape which AI tools are designed for which market segments.
Short Selling
Borrowing a security, selling it, and buying it back later at a lower price to return to the lender. Profit comes from price declines. Hedge funds use AI signals to identify short candidates systematically.
Liquidity
How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price. AI tools built for large-cap equities don't transfer to illiquid credit or private markets — the data and execution environment are fundamentally different.
ETF (Exchange-Traded Fund)
A basket of securities tracking an index, sector, or theme, traded like a stock on exchange. AI is now used to design, manage, and rebalance ETF portfolios — and thematic AI ETFs are themselves a major asset class in 2026.
