Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more terms your analysts and portfolio managers use in every investment discussion — the vocabulary that lets you challenge assumptions and ask the right product questions in discovery conversations.

Beta
A stock's sensitivity to market movements. Beta of 1.2 means it moves 20% more than the index. Portfolio managers target specific beta exposures — too high means excess risk; too low may mean underperformance.
Sharpe Ratio
Return per unit of risk (volatility). The primary measure of risk-adjusted performance. A portfolio returning 10% with low volatility can have a higher Sharpe than one returning 15% with wild swings.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A bond with duration of 7 falls ~7% in price when rates rise 1%. Fixed-income analysts live by duration — it's the primary risk measure in bond portfolios.
Credit Spread
The yield gap between a corporate bond and an equivalent government bond. Widening spreads signal rising default risk or market stress. Narrowing spreads reflect improving credit confidence. A key buy-side signal.
Benchmark
The index a portfolio is measured against — e.g. the S&P 500, FTSE All-Share, or a custom blended index. Every performance conversation starts here. AI tools are being used to decompose benchmark deviation into factors.
Volatility
The annualised standard deviation of returns — how much a price moves. High volatility means higher risk and higher potential reward. The VIX measures expected S&P 500 volatility; a rising VIX signals market fear.

BoE July FSR: Agentic AI Is Now a Systemic Risk — Cyber Vulnerability, Correlated Behaviour, AI Debt Financing

The Bank of England's July 2026 Financial Stability Report identifies agentic AI as a new systemic risk vector. Three channels: AI amplifying cyberattack sophistication and scale; AI-driven correlated investor behaviour amplifying market stress; and rapid growth in AI debt financing creating leverage concentrations in the financial system.

Why It Matters

Systemic risk isn't just a macro concern — it shapes what the PRA and FCA will scrutinise in your firm. If your product touches investment decisions, it is already in scope of how supervisors think about correlated AI behaviour and operational resilience.
