Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's edition covers concepts that appear constantly in conversations about how AI is reshaping investment decisions — from the risks your tools surface to the language your portfolio managers use to describe them.

Liquidity
How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price. AI tools that surface liquidity signals help PMs avoid trading into thin markets — a key product differentiator.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline of a portfolio or fund. July's AI selloff caused significant drawdowns for tech-heavy funds. Risk management tools focus heavily on drawdown limits and recovery time.
Volatility / VIX
Volatility measures price movement magnitude. The VIX (CBOE Volatility Index) is the market's "fear gauge." AI products must behave sensibly — and signal clearly — when volatility spikes.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Net profit divided by shares outstanding. EPS is the single most-watched number in earnings season. AI tools that surface EPS estimates, revisions, and surprises in real time are high-value.
P/E Ratio
Price-to-Earnings: what investors pay per £1 of earnings. A high P/E implies high growth expectations. AI stocks traded at elevated P/Es, making them vulnerable to the July correction.
Systematic vs. Discretionary
Systematic funds follow algorithmic rules; discretionary funds rely on human judgement. AI is blurring this line — most firms now run hybrid approaches, which shapes how they want AI tools to behave.
