Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's six terms cover the mechanics and measurements that sit beneath the headlines your analysts read every morning — from how trades are executed in the dark to how risk-adjusted returns are scored.

Basis Points (bps)
One basis point = 0.01%. Used everywhere in fixed income and finance: a 25bps rate cut, a 10bps management fee. AI tools that flag yield spreads or fee changes in bps are speaking your users' native language.
Sharpe Ratio
A risk-adjusted return metric: (return − risk-free rate) ÷ standard deviation. Higher is better. Portfolio managers use it to compare strategies on a level playing field. AI tools that surface or improve Sharpe ratios are directly valuable.
Long/Short Strategy
A hedge fund approach: buy assets expected to rise (long), sell short assets expected to fall. Returns are driven by the spread between the two legs. AI is increasingly used to identify long/short pairs at scale.
VWAP
Volume-Weighted Average Price — the average price a security has traded at throughout the day, weighted by volume. Traders use it as an execution benchmark: "we got filled at VWAP" means you matched the market average.
Dark Pool
A private exchange where large institutional trades are executed away from public markets to minimise price impact. Buy-side desks use dark pools to move large blocks without alerting the broader market — context for any product touching order routing.
Cost Basis
The original purchase price of an asset, used to calculate capital gains or losses on sale. For tax-aware portfolio management, tracking cost basis across large books is an important — and increasingly AI-assisted — operational challenge.
