Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's edition covers concepts that come up constantly in earnings calls, fund communications, and analyst workflows — the vocabulary your users live in daily.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell them now, hoping to buy them back cheaper later and pocket the difference. AI sentiment tools are increasingly used to identify short opportunities from earnings call tone and filing language.
Earnings Season
The quarterly reporting period when public companies release financial results. Q2 2026 earnings season is underway now. Analysts and PMs are most data-hungry in these weeks — your product's peak usage period.
Bid-Ask Spread
The gap between the highest price a buyer will pay (bid) and the lowest price a seller will accept (ask). A proxy for market liquidity — narrow spread means a liquid, efficient market; wide spread means risk.
Market Capitalisation
Share price multiplied by total shares outstanding. Defines whether a company is large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap — a key dimension for how your users filter and categorise investment universes.
Dividend Yield
Annual dividend per share divided by the share price. A key income metric for equity investors — especially pension funds. AI tools that forecast dividend sustainability are high-value for your buy-side users.
Volatility (VIX)
The VIX measures expected market volatility over the next 30 days — nicknamed the "fear index." When the VIX spikes, analysts' demands for real-time signal intensify: the highest-pressure moment for your product to perform.

FCA · UK

FCA Publishes Landmark Mills Review: Seven Recommendations, One Striking Consumer Stat

The 147-page Mills Review — led by FCA executive director Sheldon Mills — covers AI-driven transformation of core firm functions, agent-led consumer journeys, competition reshaping, and amplified fraud risk. The FCA will not introduce AI-specific rules: Consumer Duty and SM&CR remain the governing framework.

Why It Matters

Consumer Duty's "good outcomes" test now applies directly to AI tools that touch client-facing decisions. Explainability and auditability are not optional features — they are your regulatory baseline. Build them into the product spec, not as a retrofit.

Research & Think About
  • Read the Mills Review in full — fca.org.uk/publications/corporate-documents/mills-review — particularly the seven recommendations
  • Under SM&CR: map the named Senior Manager at your firm who would be accountable if your AI product produced a harmful output
  • Research how "agent-led consumer journeys" in the review maps to the agentic workflows you are building for internal analysts
