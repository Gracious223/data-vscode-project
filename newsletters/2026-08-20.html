Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

These are the terms your portfolio managers and analysts use daily. Each one connects to a real product decision you will face building AI tools in this space.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares to sell now, then buying them back later at a lower price to profit from a decline. AI tools that surface negative signals or deteriorating fundamentals are directly relevant to short-side strategies.
Market Maker
A firm that continuously quotes both buy and sell prices, providing liquidity. They profit from the spread. AI is increasingly used to optimise market-making inventory and quote decisions in real time.
VIX (Volatility Index)
The "fear gauge" — measures the market's expectation of 30-day S&P 500 volatility. A rising VIX signals uncertainty or stress. AI tools trained on historical VIX patterns can surface macro risk signals for portfolio managers.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A bond with duration of 7 falls ~7% if rates rise 1%. This is critical context for any AI tool helping fixed-income analysts — it shapes which rate scenarios matter most.
Sharpe Ratio
Return per unit of risk — (return minus risk-free rate) divided by volatility. The standard measure of risk-adjusted performance. Any AI product claiming to improve portfolio outcomes should be evaluated against this metric.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of one percentage point (0.01%). The standard unit for interest rates, spreads, and fees. When analysts say "spreads tightened 25bps," they mean yield differences compressed by 0.25%. You'll hear this constantly.
