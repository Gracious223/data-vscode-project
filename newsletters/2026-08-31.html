Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms tied to how analysts measure and manage portfolio risk — the language of fund management, execution, and fixed income. Understanding these lets you build products that speak your users' vocabulary, not around it.

Liquidity
How quickly and cheaply an asset can be converted to cash without moving its price. Analysts prioritise liquidity when sizing positions — illiquid assets are harder to exit and harder for AI tools to value accurately.
Drawdown
Peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value over a period. Maximum drawdown is a key risk metric — AI risk systems monitor drawdown in real time and trigger alerts when thresholds are breached.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes — measured in years. A 5-year duration bond falls ~5% in value if rates rise 1%. Fixed income analysts use duration to manage rate risk across portfolios.
Credit Spread
The yield premium a corporate bond pays over a risk-free government bond of the same maturity. Widening spreads signal rising default risk or market stress. AI models use spread movements as early warning signals.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
Total fund assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding — the per-share value of a fund. AI tools that process NAV in real time underpin automated fund administration and performance attribution.
Tracking Error
The standard deviation of a portfolio's returns relative to its benchmark — how much it deviates from the index. AI portfolio optimisers minimise tracking error in passive strategies while maximising alpha in active ones.
