Public Markets 101
Six concepts your portfolio manager and analyst users reference constantly

These terms come up daily in conversations with portfolio managers and analysts — knowing them will sharpen your product questions and make you more credible in the room.

P/E Ratio
Price divided by earnings per share — a core valuation metric. Is this stock cheap or expensive relative to what it earns? AI tools that surface real-time P/E across a portfolio at speed are baseline expectations now.
Beta
How volatile a stock is relative to the market. Beta of 1 = moves with the market; Beta of 1.5 = 50% more volatile. Portfolio managers use beta to control risk — AI increasingly models beta under stress scenarios.
Duration
A fixed-income measure of interest-rate sensitivity. A bond with 7-year duration loses ~7% in value if rates rise 1%. Critical for any fixed-income user — and directly relevant if your product touches bond portfolios.
Credit Spread
The yield premium a corporate bond pays over a risk-free government bond. Wider spreads signal perceived risk; tightening signals improving conditions. AI tools now monitor spread movements at scale across entire credit books.
Active vs. Passive
Active managers try to beat the market through stock selection. Passive funds track an index (e.g. S&P 500). AI is being applied heavily on the active side — to find alpha that passive strategies, by definition, cannot generate.
AUM
Assets Under Management — the total value of assets a firm manages on behalf of clients. A key shorthand for firm size, complexity, and influence. "We manage $40B AUM" tells you more about a firm's product needs than almost any other single number.

FINRA · US

FINRA Makes AI Governance a Formal Examination Priority — Shadow AI and Autonomous Agents in the Crosshairs

FINRA's 2026 Regulatory Oversight Report adds a dedicated AI governance section for the first time. Firms must inventory all GenAI tools, archive AI-driven decisions for recordkeeping, and demonstrate human oversight of agentic systems. Autonomous agents operating with no "human in the loop" are explicitly flagged as an emerging enforcement risk by regulators.

Why It Matters

Shadow AI — models employees use without firm approval — is now a compliance liability, not merely an IT policy issue. Your product needs to be the sanctioned option, and your PRD should document explicitly where human oversight checkpoints live in every workflow.

Research & Think About
  • Read: FINRA 2026 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report (Mayer Brown or ACA Group summary) — the AI section is four pages and worth every minute
  • How does your product's architecture support the recordkeeping requirement — can AI-generated outputs be audited post-hoc by a compliance team?
  • Define precisely what "human in the loop" means in your product — at which step can an analyst see, challenge, or override what the AI has done?
