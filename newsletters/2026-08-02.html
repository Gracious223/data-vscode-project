Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six more terms your analysts and portfolio managers use daily — the vocabulary you need to hold your own in product discovery conversations, stakeholder reviews, and strategy discussions.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares you don't own, selling them, then buying them back cheaper to profit from price falls. Short sellers act as a market check on overvalued stocks. AI tools increasingly detect short-squeeze setups.
Market Capitalisation
Share price × total shares outstanding. Used to classify companies as large-cap, mid-cap, or small-cap. A core filter in equity screening — your users live by it.
AUM (Assets Under Management)
The total market value of assets a fund or portfolio manager oversees. A key metric of scale and influence. Larger AUM increases the market-moving risk of individual trades.
Earnings Per Share (EPS)
Net profit divided by share count. One of the most-watched metrics at earnings season. AI tools are being used to forecast EPS surprises — a major edge in equities.
Liquidity
How easily an asset can be bought or sold without moving its price. Critical for institutional managers with large positions — they can't simply sell if a market is illiquid.
Drawdown
Peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio or fund's value. Managers are often assessed on maximum drawdown alongside returns — it captures risk in a way raw returns don't.
