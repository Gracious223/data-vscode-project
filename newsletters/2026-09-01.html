Lead Story · Wall Street

Goldman, JPMorgan, Citi & Barclays Are Cutting Junior Analyst Classes by Two-Thirds

The four largest investment banks are shrinking their junior analyst cohorts by up to two-thirds as AI absorbs entry-level financial modelling, document drafting, and data extraction. Jamie Dimon told JPMorgan's Q2 earnings call that AI has already reduced headcount by 30–40% in discrete areas. Banks are pulling attrition, class reductions, and redeployment as the primary levers — lowering headcount quietly while annual turnover does the rest.

Why It Matters

The analysts your product serves are a shrinking, higher-leverage cohort. Each surviving analyst must do more — which means your tool's ROI case gets stronger, but the bar for reliability gets higher too. Build for the analyst who now covers three portfolios, not one.

Research & Think About
  • Interview two analysts: what tasks have they stopped doing because AI now handles them, and what do they still refuse to delegate?
  • Read: "The Paradox of Automation" (Lisanne Bainbridge, 1983) — still the clearest framework for human-AI handoffs under pressure
  • How does headcount reduction change your internal stakeholder map? Who now owns the budget your product competes for?

Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your users think in these terms every day. Internalising them helps you ask better discovery questions, write sharper PRDs, and hold your own in a room of portfolio managers and analysts.

Beta
A measure of how sensitive a stock or portfolio is to movements in the broader market. Beta of 1 = moves with the market; above 1 = more volatile; below 1 = more defensive. AI tools increasingly help PMs manage portfolio beta in real time.
Yield Curve
A chart plotting interest rates across different bond maturities. An inverted yield curve (short rates above long rates) has historically predicted recessions. It shapes fixed income allocation decisions your users make daily.
Short Selling
Borrowing and selling a security you don't own, betting the price will fall. You buy it back cheaper to profit. AI-powered short-selling signals are a high-stakes use case: the downside is theoretically unlimited.
AUM (Assets Under Management)
The total market value of investments a firm or fund manages on behalf of clients. It's the primary metric for measuring a fund's scale. BlackRock manages ~$10T AUM — Aladdin underpins $21T globally including third-party clients.
Basis Points (bps)
One hundredth of one percent (0.01%). The standard unit for expressing interest rate changes, fees, and yield differences. A 25bps fee is 0.25%. When analysts say "it moved 50bps," they mean 0.5%.
Sharpe Ratio
Risk-adjusted return: return earned above the risk-free rate, divided by volatility. A higher Sharpe means more return per unit of risk. AI tools are being evaluated partly on whether they improve portfolio Sharpe ratios.

FCA's Mills Review: AI Will Shift Finance from Assistant to Decision-Maker by 2030

Published July 6, the landmark Mills Review — led by FCA Executive Director Sheldon Mills — maps four structural shifts by 2030: firms transformed by AI, agent-led consumer journeys, reshaped market power, and amplified cyber threats. Seven priority recommendations to the FCA. No new AI-specific regulation proposed.

Why It Matters

The FCA is signalling that Consumer Duty, SM&CR, and operational resilience rules already govern AI outputs. If your product influences a consumer financial decision, you are in scope today — not in 2030. This is not a future problem.

Research & Think About
  • Read the full Mills Review PDF at fca.org.uk — it is 80 pages and worth your Saturday morning
  • Identify which of the four structural shifts most directly affects the product you are building — what would you do differently if you took it seriously?
  • Under SM&CR: who at your firm is the named individual accountable for AI outputs? If you don't know, find out before your next PRD review
