Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Your users navigate markets using a shared vocabulary. These six terms come up daily in portfolio management and research conversations — knowing them cold lets you hold the room.

Beta
A measure of how much an asset moves relative to the market. Beta of 1 = moves with the market. Beta of 1.5 = 50% more volatile. Low-beta portfolios are "defensive." High-beta positions are directional bets.
Basis Points (bps)
The standard unit for interest rates and yield differences. 1 bps = 0.01%. A 25 bps rate cut means rates fall by 0.25%. When analysts say "spreads widened 50 bps," they mean risk premiums rose by half a percentage point.
Duration
A bond's sensitivity to interest rate changes. A bond with duration of 7 loses ~7% in price if rates rise 1%. AI tools that model rate scenarios for fixed income portfolios are working directly on this risk.
Short Selling
Borrowing a security, selling it, and hoping to buy it back cheaper later. A core hedge fund strategy. AI tools that analyse short interest and borrow rates give analysts an informational edge over the market.
Market Capitalisation
Share price × shares outstanding. Large-cap: typically >$10B. Mid-cap: $2–10B. Small-cap: <$2B. Analysts may cover all three tiers; AI research coverage expansion is most impactful in small- and mid-cap where human analyst coverage is thin.
Max Drawdown
The largest peak-to-trough decline in portfolio value over a period. A critical risk metric — a fund with a 40% max drawdown may have great returns but severe downside. AI risk tools focus heavily on modelling and minimising drawdown scenarios.

FCA · Lead Story

FCA Publishes Mills Review: Agentic AI to Reshape Retail Finance by 2030

On 6 July 2026, the FCA published its Mills Review — described as the first of its kind by any financial regulator globally. It charts how AI will move from simple digital tools to "agentic" AI acting on customers' behalf, and identifies governance, transparency, and accountability as the critical gaps firms must close. The FCA will not introduce AI-specific rules; existing Consumer Duty and SM&CR frameworks apply.

Why It Matters

The FCA has drawn the regulatory map through 2030. Governance and accountability are now your product's compliance requirements, not optional features. Every agentic AI product you ship for regulated firms must answer: who is accountable when this goes wrong?

Research & Think About
  • Read the Mills Review itself — fca.org.uk — paying particular attention to the accountability and personalisation sections
  • Under SM&CR: which Senior Manager Function at your firm would be accountable for an AI output that harms a customer?
  • Map Consumer Duty's "good outcome" definition against your product's outputs — are there gaps?
