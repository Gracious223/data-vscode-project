Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms tied to today's themes: valuation, risk measurement, and the metrics your analysts use to screen, size, and assess positions — the vocabulary you need to build credibly for their workflows.

Beta
A stock's sensitivity to market moves. Beta of 1 = moves with the market; above 1 = amplified swings; below 1 = defensive. AI portfolio tools use beta to model how a position changes overall portfolio risk.
Sharpe Ratio
Risk-adjusted return: excess return above a risk-free rate, divided by volatility. A higher Sharpe means better return per unit of risk. Key metric for evaluating AI-managed strategy performance.
Market Capitalisation
Share price × total shares outstanding. Large-cap ($10B+), mid-cap ($2–10B), small-cap (below $2B). Analysts segment their coverage universe by market cap — your product filters should mirror this.
EPS (Earnings Per Share)
Net profit divided by shares outstanding — the single number most analysts start with when screening stocks. AI earnings-forecasting tools predict EPS before results are announced.
P/E Ratio
Price divided by EPS — the most widely used valuation multiple. A high P/E signals growth expectations; a low P/E may signal value or distress. AI screening tools filter by P/E across thousands of names in seconds.
VIX (Volatility Index)
The "fear gauge" — measures expected S&P 500 volatility over 30 days. A rising VIX signals market stress. AI risk systems use VIX to dynamically adjust portfolio exposure and flag hedging triggers.

White House Finalises AI Evaluation Framework — And Won't Publish It

The White House completed its voluntary AI model evaluation framework on August 4 — reviewed by OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Meta, Microsoft, and Nvidia — and then declined to release it publicly. Benchmarks and model thresholds are classified. Open-source models are excluded from scope. The framework governs a mandatory 30-day pre-release review window for frontier AI models.

Why It Matters

A secret model evaluation standard means frontier AI vendors now operate under government oversight your firm cannot inspect. When selecting foundation model partners, the governance and policy track record of labs matters as much as benchmark scores.
