Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Today's stories touch agentic hedge funds, institutional infrastructure, and post-trade operations. Here are six terms from that world that sharpen your credibility with portfolio managers and engineers alike.

Long/Short Equity
A strategy where a manager buys stocks expected to rise (long) and sells borrowed stocks expected to fall (short). Lumenai's agentic fund uses this approach, targeting returns with low market correlation.
Correlation
A measure of how two assets move together. Low correlation to the market means a fund's returns are driven by its own skill, not market direction. Central to why Lumenai pitches its agentic architecture as differentiated.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
The per-share value of a fund's assets minus liabilities. For hedge funds, NAV is calculated daily or monthly — it's how performance is tracked and how investors know what their stake is worth.
Order Management System (OMS)
The software through which a fund routes and tracks trade orders. Arcesium wires its execution agents directly into OMS — meaning AI-generated signals reach the market through the fund's existing infrastructure.
Settlement
The process of completing a trade — transferring securities and cash between buyer and seller. Settlement failures (breaks) are a major source of operational risk. Broadridge's agentic AI resolves settlement breaks autonomously.
Alternative Data
Non-traditional datasets used to generate investment signals: satellite imagery, web traffic, card spend, job postings. AI-driven funds increasingly use it to find edges that traditional financial data no longer offers.
