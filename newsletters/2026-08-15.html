Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Each edition rotates six terms your analysts and portfolio managers use daily. Understanding these gives you the fluency to hold your own in product conversations where market expertise is assumed.

AUM (Assets Under Management)
Total market value of investments a firm manages on behalf of clients. BlackRock's $10T AUM makes Aladdin's $25T coverage (which includes third-party clients) one of the most consequential AI deployments in finance.
Beta
Measures how sensitive a stock or portfolio is to broader market movements. Beta of 1.0 = moves with the market. Above 1.0 = more volatile; below 1.0 = more defensive. AI tools use beta in real-time risk monitoring.
Drawdown
The peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value. A 20% drawdown means the portfolio fell 20% from its high point. AI tools that limit drawdown — by flagging risk earlier — are among the most valued applications in risk management.
Tracking Error
The divergence between a portfolio's returns and its benchmark. Low tracking error = staying close to the index. High = active bets. AI is used to monitor and predict tracking error in near real-time for fund managers.
Systematic / Quant Strategy
Investment approaches driven by algorithms and statistical models rather than human discretion. Two Sigma, D.E. Shaw, and Man Group run systematic strategies. AI is increasingly the engine powering this approach across the industry.
NAV (Net Asset Value)
Per-share value of a fund: total assets minus liabilities, divided by shares outstanding. Agentic AI systems are being deployed to reconcile NAV in real time across fund accounting — one of the highest-frequency, highest-stakes automation use cases.

EU AI Act · Enforcement

EU AI Act High-Risk Deadline Has Passed — Credit Scoring and Fraud Detection Are Now Regulated

August 2, 2026: the EU AI Act's full compliance obligations for high-risk AI systems became enforceable. Credit scoring, fraud detection, and automated financial decision-making are explicitly classified as high-risk. Penalties under Article 99 reach €15 million or 3% of global annual turnover. Any AI system whose output touches the EU is in scope.

Why It Matters

If your product feeds outputs into credit or investment decisions touching EU users, explainability, human oversight, and conformity documentation are now legal obligations — not product nice-to-haves. This changes your PRD from day one.

Research & Think About
  • Read Finextra: "The EU AI Act's August 2026 Deadline: What Financial Services Firms Must Do Now" — it is specific and actionable
  • Understand Articles 9–17 (provider requirements) and Article 26 (deployer requirements) — know which applies to your firm
  • Research what a "conformity assessment" requires for a high-risk AI system and how it maps to your product documentation practices
