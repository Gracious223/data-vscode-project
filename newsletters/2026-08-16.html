Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six terms your analysts and portfolio managers use daily — and that you need to hold your own in the room. Each one connects directly to an AI product decision.

Prime Brokerage
A suite of services banks provide to hedge funds: securities lending, margin financing, custody, and clearing. Understanding prime brokerage relationships helps you grasp how large funds access leverage and why data flows matter operationally.
Market Maker
Firms that stand ready to buy and sell securities at quoted prices, providing liquidity. AI is transforming market-making, enabling tighter spreads and faster price discovery. Relevant when your analysts discuss execution quality.
Convexity
How a bond's price sensitivity (duration) changes as interest rates move. Bonds with positive convexity gain more when rates fall than they lose when rates rise — a key concept in fixed income portfolio construction.
Free Float
The proportion of a company's shares available for public trading, excluding insider and locked-up holdings. Index composition and stock liquidity depend on it — AI tools use free float data to assess tradability in real time.
VWAP
Volume Weighted Average Price — the average price weighted by trading volume, used by institutional traders to execute large orders without moving the market. AI-powered execution algorithms are built around optimising VWAP performance.
Covenant
A condition in a bond or loan agreement restricting borrower behaviour — e.g. limits on leverage or asset sales. Credit analysts monitor covenant compliance continuously; AI tools that flag covenant breaches early are a high-value use case in credit research.

Deployment Reality · Bank of England / FCA

75% of UK Financial Firms Use AI — But Only 2% of Use Cases Are Fully Autonomous

A joint Bank of England and FCA survey finds 75% of UK firms use AI (94% among international banks); yet only 2% of use cases involve fully autonomous decision-making. 55% involve some automation, but the majority remain constrained or supervised. The production gap is real.

Why It Matters

The gap between pilot and production is your primary product opportunity. Most firms have the intent and the tooling — they lack the workflow integration, governance, and trust required to go fully live. That is your product's job to solve.

Research & Think About
  • Read the LLRX August 15 roundup — it consolidates the Bank of England and FCA survey findings with peer firm examples
  • Understand "human-in-the-loop" vs. "human-on-the-loop" — the distinction defines what your product can and cannot automate in a regulated context
  • Research why 98% of AI use cases in finance retain human oversight — is it regulation, trust, liability, or all three?
