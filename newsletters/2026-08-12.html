Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six concepts your portfolio manager and analyst users reference constantly — and that will help you ask sharper product questions in the room.

Short Selling
Borrowing shares you don't own, selling them, then buying them back at a lower price. A way to profit from anticipated declines — but with theoretically unlimited downside if prices rise.
Market Capitalisation
Share price × shares outstanding. "Large-cap" (>$10B), "mid-cap" ($2–10B), "small-cap" (<$2B). Size affects liquidity, volatility, and which analysts cover a stock.
Yield Curve
A plot of bond yields across different maturities (2yr, 10yr, 30yr). An "inverted" yield curve — short-term rates exceeding long-term — is a classic recession signal closely watched by PMs.
Basis Points (bps)
1 basis point = 0.01%. Used to measure interest rate and yield changes. "The Fed cut 50 bps" = a 0.5% rate cut. Table-stakes vocabulary for any fixed income or macro conversation.
Drawdown
Peak-to-trough decline in a portfolio's value. A 20% drawdown means the portfolio has fallen 20% from its high point. Portfolio managers track maximum drawdown as a core risk constraint.
Liquidity Risk
The risk that a position cannot be sold quickly without moving the price. AI products serving buy-side teams must account for the fact that not all holdings can be exited on demand — especially in credit.

FCA · UK

FCA's Mills Review Deepens — Principles-Based Oversight Holds as AI Reshapes Retail

The FCA's January 2026 "Mills Review" — examining how AI could reshape retail financial services — is now gathering evidence across four themes: future AI evolution, market and firm impact, consumer effects, and how UK regulators must adapt. The FCA, PRA, and Bank of England continue to rely on Consumer Duty and SM&CR rather than bespoke AI rules.

Why It Matters

Consumer Duty still requires demonstrable "good outcomes." Explainability and auditability are not optional extras — they are the compliance floor for any AI product your firm deploys to retail-adjacent users.

Research & Think About
  • Read the FCA's Consumer Duty guidance — how does "good outcome" translate into your product's eval criteria?
  • Under SM&CR, identify who at your firm is accountable when an AI output causes harm — this should be in your PRD
  • Check: does the Mills Review's terms of reference include buy-side or only retail financial services?

AI PMs in 2026: Judgment Is the Last Irreplaceable Skill

AI literacy is now table stakes — 59% of product professionals rate strategy and business acumen as the most critical PM skills for 2026. AI automates research, documentation, and analysis. Why It Matters
