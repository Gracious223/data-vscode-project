Public Markets 101
Essential concepts for your role

Six concepts you need to hold your own in conversations about market structure, valuation, and how capital moves — directly relevant to today's stories about bank earnings, analyst workflows, and AI-driven capital allocation.

P/E Ratio
Price-to-Earnings: share price divided by earnings per share. The most-watched valuation metric in equities. AI companies with high P/E ratios are priced for future growth — a key input in the investment theses your analysts build.
Beta
A stock's sensitivity to market movements. Beta of 1 means it moves with the market; above 1 means more volatile. AI stocks typically carry high beta — relevant when your PM users are managing risk in an AI-heavy portfolio.
Basis Points (bps)
One basis point = 0.01%. The unit analysts and traders use for small but meaningful changes in yields, spreads, or fees. "The spread widened 25 bps" is fluent finance-speak you'll hear in every credit discussion.
Dark Pools
Private trading venues where large institutional orders are matched without public disclosure. Major banks operate them to avoid moving the market. AI is being used to optimise order routing across dark pools and lit exchanges.
Yield Curve
A graph plotting bond yields against maturities (2yr, 10yr, 30yr). The shape signals economic expectations — a normal upward curve suggests growth; an inverted curve (short rates above long) historically precedes recessions.
Sector Rotation
Moving capital from one market sector to another in anticipation of economic cycle changes. When AI-related capital flows dominate IG issuance (as Apollo noted), sector rotation signals are one of the most valuable things an AI tool can surface quickly.
